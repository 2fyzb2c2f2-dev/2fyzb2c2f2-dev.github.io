Hermes Agents

A personal, single-user household assistant.

What this application is

Hermes Agents is a self-hosted assistant that runs on its owner's own private server. It is used by exactly one person: the owner of the Google Account that authorises it. There is no sign-up, no other users, and no service offered to the public.

What it does with Google data

The owner authorises the assistant to use these Google services on the owner's own account only:

  • Gmail — to find incoming invoices, bills and contracts, and to prepare replies that the owner reviews and sends. Nothing is sent without the owner's explicit approval.
  • Google Drive — to file those documents into the owner's own Drive folder structure, and to keep encrypted backups of the assistant's own local settings and records.
  • Google Calendar — to create and read the owner's own events, such as travel bookings and reminders.
  • Google Sheets and Docs — to keep the household's own records and generated documents.
  • Contacts — read-only, to recognise who a document came from.

How the data is handled

All data stays between the owner's Google Account and the owner's own private server. It is not sent to the assistant's author, to any third party, or to any external service, and it is never sold or used for advertising. Documents and records are stored on the owner's own hardware, and encrypted backups are stored in the owner's own Drive. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page, after which the assistant can no longer read anything.

Contact
