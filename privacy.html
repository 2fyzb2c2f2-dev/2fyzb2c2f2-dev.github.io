Privacy policy

Hermes Agents — last updated 15 November 2026

Hermes Agents ("the application") is a private, self-hosted assistant with a single user: the person who installed it and who authorises it against their own Google Account ("the owner"). This policy describes how the application accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

1. What Google user data the application accesses

With the owner's consent, and only for the owner's own Google Account, the application accesses:

  • Gmail — message metadata, message bodies and attachments of the owner's own mailbox, in order to identify and file documents such as invoices, bills and contracts, and to prepare draft replies for the owner's review.
  • Google Drive — files and folders in the owner's own Drive, in order to store the documents it files and encrypted backups of its own records.
  • Google Calendar — the owner's own calendars, to create and read events such as travel bookings and reminders.
  • Google Sheets and Google Docs — spreadsheets and documents owned by the owner, used as the household's own record-keeping.
  • Contacts — read-only, to identify the sender of a document. The application does not create, modify or delete contacts.

The application does not access data belonging to anyone other than the owner, and does not act on behalf of any other Google Account.

2. How the data is used

Google user data is used solely to provide these functions to the owner: filing the owner's documents, summarising what needs the owner's attention, keeping the household's records, maintaining the owner's calendar, and preparing messages that the owner reviews before anything is sent. The application never sends mail without the owner's explicit approval for that specific message.

3. How the data is stored

Documents, records and derived state are stored on the owner's own private server and in the owner's own Google Drive. Backup copies are encrypted before they are uploaded. The application's access credentials are stored on the owner's own server with restricted file permissions.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not shared, transferred or disclosed to the application's author or to any third party. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising, profiling or training machine-learning models. No human other than the owner reads it. Data is disclosed only if the owner explicitly instructs the application to send something, and only to the recipient the owner names.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only for as long as the owner keeps it. The owner can delete filed documents and records at any time, directly in their own Google Drive or on their own server. Revoking the application's access in the owner's Google Account permissions page immediately ends all further access; the owner may then also delete the application's local data at their discretion.

6. Security

Access tokens are stored on the owner's own server with restrictive permissions, and the server is not publicly accessible. Data in transit to and from Google uses HTTPS.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. Because the application has a single user, the owner is also the person responsible for it.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or about Google user data handled by the application go to the address given as the application's support contact on its authorisation screen.
